# Example SaaS Services Agreement

This example is fictional and is included only to test the legal assistant.

## 1. Term

The Agreement begins on 1 August 2026 and remains in effect for one year. It renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives at least 60 days' written notice before the end of the then-current term.

## 2. Fees and payment

Customer will pay the fees in each order form within 30 days after the invoice date. Overdue undisputed amounts accrue interest at 1% per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is lower.

## 3. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach if the breaching party does not cure the breach within 15 days after written notice. Provider may suspend the service immediately for an undisputed invoice more than 30 days overdue. Customer may terminate for convenience on 90 days' written notice, but prepaid fees are non-refundable.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party must use reasonable care to protect Confidential Information and may use it only to perform this Agreement. These duties survive for five years after termination; trade-secret duties survive while the information remains a trade secret.

## 5. Liability

Except for fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality breaches, and indemnification obligations, each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid during the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect, special, or consequential damages.

## 6. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Karnataka, India. Courts located in Bengaluru have exclusive jurisdiction.
